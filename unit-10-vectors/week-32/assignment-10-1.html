📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
This set drills the vocabulary of a vector: writing component form from two points, computing magnitude, and finding a direction angle with the quadrant correction the lesson practiced. This is a practice set: scored for honest completion, with worked solutions released after the due date for line-by-line self-correction.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
A calculator's \(\tan^{-1}\) key only ever answers with an angle between \(-90^\circ\) and \(90^\circ\). What has to happen after you press that key before you can trust the direction angle it gives you?
📝 PROBLEM SET

Show your work in neat vertical form — state the reference angle and the quadrant reasoning explicitly for every direction-angle problem, not just the final angle.

1
Component form from two points: Write \(\overrightarrow{PQ}\) in component form for \(P(1,2)\) and \(Q(4,6)\), and find its magnitude.
2
Magnitude: Find the magnitude of \(\mathbf{v}=\langle-6,8\rangle\).
3
Direction angle, Quadrant II: Find the direction angle of \(\mathbf{v}=\langle-1,1\rangle\), stating the reference angle and your quadrant reasoning.
4
Direction angle, on an axis: Find the direction angle of \(\mathbf{v}=\langle0,-5\rangle\). (No reference-angle triangle is needed here — explain why, using the picture of where this vector points.)
5
Explain why: Two different vectors, one in Quadrant II and one in Quadrant IV, could have the exact same reference angle. Explain why their direction angles must still come out different, and how you would tell them apart.
6
Honors stretch: A drone flies from \(A(3,-2)\) to \(B(-1,5)\). Write \(\overrightarrow{AB}\) in component form, and find its exact magnitude (leave it as a radical) and its direction angle to the nearest hundredth of a degree.
💡 CHECK YOURSELF WITH DESMOS
For Problem 6, plot (3, -2) and (-1, 5) in desmos.com/calculator, connect them with the segment or vector tool, and use distance((3,-2),(-1,5)) to confirm your exact magnitude numerically.

This practice set is scored for completion (10 pts): full credit means every problem is attempted with visible work. After the due date, worked solutions are released — compare your work line by line, mark each miss, and redo it. That self-correction is the real assignment.

The standard below governs all written work in this course, all year. Hold your pages to it now; it is exactly the rubric your teacher applies on Focus assignments and unit tests.

Standard of Work What it looks like on the page
Understanding of the material Component forms are computed by subtracting initial from terminal coordinates; magnitudes apply the distance formula correctly; direction angles land in the correct quadrant.
Quality of reasoning Quadrant choices are justified by reasoning about the signs of the components, not asserted; Problem 5's explanation reasons from the picture.
Presentation & precision Work is neatly organized; exact radicals are left exact unless a problem asks for a decimal.
Intellectual honesty Work shown faithfully; gaps and uncertainty named rather than bluffed.
