📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
This is Week 29's Focus assignment and the capstone of Unit 8: two full translation problems where a single polar curve must be rewritten in both rectangular and parametric form, with every claimed point checked against all three descriptions at once. The two gold-badged ⭐ METHOD PROBLEMS are complete translation reports, and your teacher reads them the way this whole unit has trained: every conversion technique named as it is used, every verification shown, every claim checked rather than assumed. Give every problem honest work, and give the method problems the complete documentation discipline — nothing skipped because it felt obvious.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
A single curve can be written three honest ways. What test would convince a skeptic that all three descriptions really do trace the exact same set of points?
📝 PROBLEM SET

Show your work for every problem in neat vertical form — one step per line, equals signs aligned, labeled answers. On the method problems, name every conversion technique before you use it, and verify every claimed point against all three forms.

1
Warmup — polar to rectangular: Convert \(r=4\sin\theta\) to rectangular form and identify the curve completely (center and radius).
2
Warmup — parametric to rectangular: Eliminate the parameter from \(x=3+2\cos t,\ y=-1+2\sin t\) and identify the curve completely.
⭐ METHOD PROBLEM — your teacher grades this one for method
3
One curve, three languages. A curve is given in polar form: \(r=4\cos\theta\). ① Convert to rectangular, naming the technique (multiply both sides by \(r\), then complete the square), and identify the circle's center and radius. ② Derive a parametric form by substituting \(r=4\cos\theta\) directly into \(x=r\cos\theta,\ y=r\sin\theta\), using \(\theta\) itself as the parameter, and simplify using a double-angle identity. State the domain of \(\theta\) needed to trace the curve exactly once. ③ Verify all three forms agree at \(\theta=\dfrac{\pi}{4}\): compute the point from the polar form, confirm it satisfies your rectangular equation, and confirm your parametric form produces the identical point at the matching parameter value. ④ Close with two or three sentences: which of the three forms most quickly reveals that this circle passes through the pole, and why?
⭐ METHOD PROBLEM — your teacher grades this one for method
4
The drone's holding pattern. A search-and-rescue drone flies a circular holding pattern of radius \(6\) nautical miles, centered on a rescue beacon at the pole, completing one loop every \(2\pi\) minutes, starting due east of the beacon. ① Write the parametric equations for the drone's position, with \(t\) in minutes. ② Write the polar equation of the flight path, and explain in one sentence why it takes this especially simple form. ③ Write the rectangular equation of the flight path. ④ At \(t=5\) minutes, find the drone's position in rectangular coordinates (calculator permitted for the trigonometric evaluation; \(t\) is in radians), rounded to three decimal places, and verify your point satisfies your rectangular equation from ③. ⑤ Close with one sentence: what does the parametric form tell you about the drone's position that the polar equation, by itself, cannot?
5
The AI cross-examination: Give your finished problem-4 work to your AI tutor with the instruction: “Check my parametric-to-rectangular reasoning for this circular flight path and tell me whether my position at \(t=5\) is correct.” Copy the AI's response onto your page (two or three sentences of it), then write your own verdict: is the AI's check correct? Verify every claim it makes before you rule — if it catches something real, say what you fixed; if its check is itself wrong, refute it with your own recomputation. Either outcome earns full credit; what is graded is the judging.
6
Honors stretch — a second circle through the pole: Repeat Method Problem 3's technique on the polar curve \(r=8\sin\theta\): ① convert to rectangular form (center and radius), ② derive the parametric form using \(\theta\) as the parameter, and ③ verify all three forms agree at \(\theta=\dfrac{\pi}{4}\).
💡 CHECK YOURSELF WITH DESMOS
For problem 3, graph \(r=4\cos\theta\) directly in Desmos polar mode (desmos.com/calculator) and, separately, graph your parametric form from ② — the two curves should sit exactly on top of each other. For problem 4, graph the parametric drone path and trace to the \(t=5\) point to confirm it lands where your hand computation says.

📊 GRADING RUBRIC

Your teacher applies this rubric to the two ⭐ method problems (the rest of the set is checked for honest completion). This same four-criterion standard governs every hand-graded item in this course, all year.

Criteria Excellent (4) Proficient (3) Developing (2) Beginning (1) Pts
Understanding of the material
4 pts		 All three representations are derived correctly, and every conversion technique is applied without error. Method is sound with a minor slip that does not derail the translation. Partial grasp; the approach starts correctly but loses the thread on one representation. The method does not match the mathematics being asked. 4 pts
Quality of reasoning
4 pts		 Every step follows from the last; techniques are named as they are used, and the cross-representation verification is actually performed, not merely asserted. Reasoning is mostly explicit; one link in the chain is asserted rather than shown. Steps are present but read as recipe-following; explanations restate what without why. Answer appears with little or no supporting reasoning. 4 pts
Presentation & precision
4 pts		 Clean vertical layout, aligned equals signs, labeled answers throughout; the report reads as a document a stranger could act on. Organized and legible with small lapses in labeling or alignment. Readable but disorderly; the grader must hunt for the thread. Work is scattered or illegible; steps cannot be followed. 4 pts
Intellectual honesty
4 pts		 All work shown faithfully as done; uncertainty and gaps are named plainly; the AI verdict tells the whole truth, including if something seemed off. Work is genuine; one rough patch is smoothed over rather than acknowledged. Some steps appear reverse-engineered from the answer rather than worked. The page misrepresents how the work was actually done. 4 pts
